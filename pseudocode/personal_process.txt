Problem:
Every weekday morning I get ready and leave home for class. I need to make sure I have everything and arrive on time.

Class timetable for the day, my backpack, laptop, charger, notebook, phone, and a charged phone battery.

Output:
I leave home on time with everything I need and arrive at class before it starts.

Edge Case Handled:
My phone or laptop battery is low and there is a power cut at home.

Pack notebook, laptop, charger and phone into my backpack
Check the battery level of my phone and laptop

    END IF
ELSE
    Continue to the next step
END IF

END

Each step is a single action in the order I do it, so another person can follow it without guessing. The process starts with checking the timetable and ends with leaving for class, so it is clear where it begins and finishes.
